Teaching

Université Clermont Auvergne

Impact Analysis

Teaching assistant · 2nd-year M.Sc. · 2025–present

Graduate course on the design and econometrics of impact evaluation. I lead the applied sessions, in which students take randomized experiments, difference-in-differences, instrumental-variable, and regression-discontinuity designs from identification assumptions to implementation on real data.

Development Economics Seminar

Teaching assistant · 2nd-year M.Sc. · 2025–present

Research seminar in which students read, present, and discuss recent empirical work in development economics. I supervise student presentations and guide the critical discussion of research designs and results.

Econometrics

Teaching assistant · 1st-year M.Sc. · 2025–present

Core graduate course covering the linear regression model, statistical inference, and the treatment of endogeneity. I run the tutorial sessions, combining problem sets with hands-on estimation exercises.
