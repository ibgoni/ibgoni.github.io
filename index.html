Welcome. I am a Ph.D. candidate in development economics at CERDI (Université Clermont Auvergne, CNRS, IRD) in Clermont-Ferrand, France, under the supervision of Vianney Dequiedt and Pierre Biscaye.

My research studies how digital infrastructure shapes economic development in Sub-Saharan Africa: how the arrival of broadband internet changes local economic activity, and how the reliability of international connectivity conditions workers’ access to global online labor markets. In related work, I examine the robustness of willingness-to-pay elicitation in field experiments and the transmission of climate shocks through agricultural markets.
