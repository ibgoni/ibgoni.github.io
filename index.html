Ph.D. Candidate in Development Economics

Ibrahim Goni Abdoulkadiri

CERDI — Université Clermont Auvergne, CNRS, IRD

Welcome. I am a Ph.D. candidate in development economics at CERDI (Université Clermont Auvergne, CNRS, IRD) in Clermont-Ferrand, France, under the supervision of Vianney Dequiedt and Pierre Biscaye.

My research interests lie in development economics and applied microeconomics. I am broadly interested in how infrastructure, technology, and markets shape economic opportunities in low- and middle-income countries. My current work includes projects on digital connectivity, labor markets, agricultural markets, and field experiments, with a particular focus on Sub-Saharan Africa.

You can find my working papers and ongoing projects on the research page, my tutorial materials on the teaching page, and download my CV (PDF).
